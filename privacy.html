Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

VibeFlow ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you use our music player application, and just as importantly, what we do not.

The short version: VibeFlow does not collect your personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking. Your music library, playlists, and settings are stored only on your device.

1. Introduction

VibeFlow is a music player application focused on simple playback, playlist management, and a smooth listening experience. By using VibeFlow, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Handle

VibeFlow is designed to minimize data handling. The following information is generated and used entirely on your own device, and is never uploaded to us or to any server we control:

  • Your music library and imported files;
  • Playlists, favorites, and playback history;
  • App settings and preferences;
  • In-app cache required for playback.

No account or registration is required to use VibeFlow. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, microphone audio, or camera data.

3. No Analytics, No Advertising, No Tracking

VibeFlow does not include any analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not engage in cross-app tracking. Specifically:

  • We do not collect device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA);
  • We do not request permission through Apple's AppTrackingTransparency framework;
  • We do not display advertisements or share data with advertising networks;
  • We do not use third-party analytics services to measure your behavior.

4. Third-Party Content

VibeFlow may let you access music, cover artwork, lyrics, or related media from third-party content providers or public sources. When the app retrieves such content on your behalf:

  • The app sends only what is necessary to fetch the public content you requested (for example, a search query or a content identifier);
  • These requests do not include your personal data or device identifiers;
  • These third-party platforms operate under their own independent privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Security

Network requests made by the app (for example, to fetch public music metadata) are transmitted over encrypted secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS). As is true for any internet-connected app, no transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, but we use standard protections where network access occurs.

6. Local Data and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, it remains under your control. You can clear cached content at any time from within the app. Uninstalling VibeFlow completely removes all data stored on your device — there is no server-side copy to delete because we never received one.

7. Children's Privacy

VibeFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, we do not collect data from children. If you believe otherwise, please contact us.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Since VibeFlow does not collect your personal data, there is no server-side profile to access, edit, or delete. You remain in full control through your device:

  • Manage local data: Review or clear your library, playlists, and cache within the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Stop using the app: Uninstalling VibeFlow removes all locally stored data.
  • Contact us: For any privacy-related questions, reach out directly using the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
